# AMD Radeon RX 7800 XT (16 GB, `gfx1101`): Nib evaluation of NVFP4-23 vs NVFP4-All

Real-silicon evaluation of **Nib** running **Inkling 2 Mini** in two NVFP4 repacks on an **AMD Radeon RX 7800 XT** (`gfx1101` / Navi 32, 60 CU / 30 WGP, 256-bit, 624 GB/s, 64 MiB Infinity Cache) under Windows/WSL2 (`ROCDXG`, ROCm 7.2.4), tested on **2026-11-26**:

- **NVFP4-23**: experts in layers 0–22 are NVFP4 and layer 23 stays MXFP8→Q8_0. Size 16,099,143,008 B; 14.46 GiB of weights on GPU with `--cpu-embd`.
- **NVFP4-All**: experts in every layer (0–23) are NVFP4. Size 15,589,536,192 B; 13.98 GiB of weights on GPU with `--cpu-embd`.

Every number below is recorded in [`benchmarks.json`](benchmarks.json) and the BetterBench reports in this folder.

> **TL;DR**
> - **NVFP4-All is the 16 GB quant.** Both quants decode at the same speed (**~97–98 tok/s** at d0, **~93 tok/s** at depth; BetterBench weighted **98.0 vs 97.8 tok/s**). NVFP4-All frees **~0.48 GiB**, and on a 16 GB card that is the difference between a usable and an unusable context:
>   - Max clean context, no spill: **69,632 vs 20,480 (q4_0)**, **65,536 vs 16,384 (q4_1)**, **32,768 vs 8,192 (q8_0)**.
>   - Concurrency: up to **`--slots 4` at 45K and `--slots 8` at 12K** on NVFP4-All. NVFP4-23 tops out at `--slots 2` with 12K.
>   - Vision: **works on NVFP4-All only** (1080p up to 20K context, 1440p up to 8K context).
> - **DSpark MTP does not fit on either quant on 16 GB.** `DSparkDrafter: hipMalloc failed` even at `-c 2048 --draft-ctx 1024`. The drafter needs ~0.68 GiB on top of a ≥14.43 GiB engine, which exceeds the measured 15.30 GiB HIP ceiling minus HIP runtime. So there are **no MTP sweeps**. This is measured, not estimated.
> - **0 MB GTT spill across every reported config.** `USED_GTT` peaked at **526–539 MB** in all BetterBench runs. That is the intended 469 MiB `--cpu-embd` pinned-host table plus the ~40–60 MB idle baseline, and it did not grow during the runs.
> - **Best NVFP4-All concurrency** (`--slots 8 -c 12288 q4_1`): **174.9 tok/s aggregate at c = 8** (22.9 tok/s per stream). At `--slots 4 -c 45056 q4_0` it reaches 159.4 tok/s aggregate at c = 4, with 32/32 OK at every level.
> - **Long-context prefill on NVFP4-All q4_0 @ 69,632:** 2,365 tok/s `pp512` and 93.5 tok/s `tg128` at 68,608 depth. The BetterBench prefill ladder reached 53,112 prompt tokens at 2,471 tok/s.

## 2. VRAM ceiling

### Stage 1: raw HIP (`hipMalloc` + `hipMemset`, touched)

- Idle free: **15,367.3 MiB** (desktop holding 809.5 MiB).
- **Hard ceiling: 15,664.0 MiB (15.2969 GiB).** Identical for 979 × 16 MiB and 416 × 32 MiB + refinement (0 MiB fragmentation loss).
- WDDM demotes the desktop under pressure: `used` was 240.9 MiB after freeing.

### Stage 2: real `nib serve` ceiling matrix

**Method.** `probe.py` boots `nib serve --cpu-embd --sparse-pages 224 [--no-vision|--mmproj]` and waits for `/health`. It then runs:
- a 128-token greedy decode;
- with `--slots N`, N concurrent 64-token requests;
- with vision, a 1080p image request and then a post-image text decode.

`amd-smi` is sampled every 0.5 s throughout, and the server is torn down afterwards.

**Clean** means three things:
1. the verdict is `PASS`;
2. GTT rise minus 469 MiB `cpu_embd` stays within 96 MB (the calibration baseline was +41 MB);
3. no OOM or HTTP 400 occurred.

`search.py` bisects `-c` in 4096 steps, from 4096 up to 131072 (262,848 was never reachable). In total 136 probes ran; all are in [`benchmarks.json`](benchmarks.json).

| Config (no MTP) | **NVFP4-All** max clean `-c` | engine | **NVFP4-23** max clean `-c` | engine |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| `q4_0` `--slots 1` | **69,632** | 15.03 GiB | **20,480** | 15.07 GiB |
| `q4_1` `--slots 1` | **65,536** | 15.06 GiB | **16,384** | 15.05 GiB |
| `q8_0` `--slots 1` | **32,768** | 15.01 GiB | **8,192** | 15.08 GiB |
| `q4_0` `--slots 2` | **65,536** | 15.06 GiB | **12,288** | 15.06 GiB |
| `q4_1` `--slots 2` | **57,344** | 15.06 GiB | **12,288** | 15.09 GiB |
| `q4_0` `--slots 4` | **45,056** | 15.02 GiB | does not fit (OOM at 4096) | — |
| `q4_1` `--slots 4` | **40,960** | 15.05 GiB | does not fit | — |
| `q4_0` / `q4_1` `--slots 8` | **12,288** (1,536/slot) | 15.01 / 15.08 GiB | does not fit | — |
| `q4_0` + Vision (1080p) | **20,480** | 14.59 GiB | does not fit (`vision_encoder.cpp:314` OOM at 4096 and 2048) | — |
| `q4_1` + Vision (1080p) | **16,384** | 14.58 GiB | does not fit | — |
| **Any KV + MTP** | **does not fit** (14.45 GiB @ 4096, 14.43 GiB @ 2048 + `--draft-ctx 1024`) | — | **does not fit** (14.92 / 14.90 GiB) | — |

- **The effective ceiling is ~15.0–15.08 GiB of nib engine VRAM** (`nib` reports ~0.18–0.30 GiB device free there), or ~15.6 GiB device used. One step higher, the boot OOMs (`engine.cpp:145`).
- **NVFP4-23 has no headroom.** At `-c 4096` it already uses 14.92 GiB. Every extra feature, whether another slot past 2, the mmproj (0.32 GiB + ViT workspace) or MTP (0.68 GiB), runs out of memory.
- **MTP math.** The smallest engine is 14.43 GiB, plus 0.68 GiB of drafter, which gives ≈15.11 GiB. Add ~0.3 GiB of HIP runtime and code objects, and that is more than 15.30 GiB. MTP on this card would need a smaller drafter, or a host-resident drafter or LM head.

- **4K and 2560² never fit on 16 GB,** not even at a 4K context and not with `--no-graph`. **1440p needs a context of 8K or less.**
